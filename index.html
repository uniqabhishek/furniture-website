Our story

It started because flat-pack furniture kept falling apart.

Grain & Joint started as one workbench in a rented garage, built out of frustration with furniture that didn't survive a single move. Today it's a team of nine, still cutting every joint by hand, still refusing to laminate a tabletop.

We keep the backlog long on purpose — six to ten weeks, depending on the piece. That's what lets us say no to shortcuts: no staples in a joint, no particleboard behind a walnut veneer, nothing we wouldn't want to repair ourselves in twenty years.
